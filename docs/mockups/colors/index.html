Editor colors: readability stress tests

Seven stress tests, written in the editor's own highlighting (app/highlight.ts), shown in the current scheme and three candidates. Switch light / dark and simulate color-vision deficiencies with the bar below. The scorecard is measured: WCAG contrast of every token against the background (4.5 is the minimum for text) and ΔE (CIE76) between roles a reader must tell apart (below 12 they look alike, above 20 they are clearly different).

Theme Vision

What the stress tests show in the current scheme

  • Structure is hard to scan (test 1). Step lines, transitions and action lines have the same weight of color; the transition marker -- is purple, but two thin dashes read as gray, so transitions don't stand out.
  • Steps lose their color where they are used (tests 2, 7). cycle_run is orange where it is defined but plain text in cycle_run.DN, while the member .DN gets mustard: the eye goes to the member, not to the step. A step and a tag (fill / fill_valve) look the same.
  • Too many hues (12). Purple, blue, orange, green, teal, navy, mustard, violet each mean something different; ST keywords (blue, bold) compete with qSFC keywords (purple, bold) in dense conditions.
  • Close pairs. Strings (navy) vs ST keywords (blue), and functions (violet) vs keywords (purple) are close; under deuteranopia, labels (green) and definitions (orange) get close too (scorecard).
  • Squiggles (test 6) stay visible, but a red error under an orange/brown definition is weak.

  • Current: 12 colors, one per token kind. Steps colored only where defined.
  • A · Calm: 4 hues for meaning: structure (indigo), steps everywhere they appear (amber), literals (teal), comments (gray). Tags, members and ST keywords stay text color; bold carries the rest. (scorecard only)
  • B · Calm + structure: A's colors, plus line roles: step lines get a tinted band and a bar in the margin, transition lines a thin bar, branch lines are muted.
  • C · Color-blind safe: Okabe-Ito hues (blue, vermillion, bluish green, reddish purple), chosen to stay apart for every color-vision deficiency. Tag members keep their own hue. (scorecard only)
  • D · Colored by role: Each kind of word gets the color of what it does in the chart: steps amber (init, step, every use of a step), transitions blue (--, names, ->), branches violet (select, parallel, |, end), actions green (on, set, reset, qualifiers, call, :=, SFR), time teal (durations, after, .done/.time), values rose. Declarations (program, tag, io) stay text-colored and extra bold, types and addresses gray; logic words text-colored bold. With B's line structure.
  • E · By role, color-blind safe: D's roles with four Okabe-Ito hues (vermillion steps, blue transitions, green actions, reddish-purple branches). Time takes its step's vermillion in italic (a preset belongs to its step); values are italic text. Fewer hues, so nothing merges under color-vision deficiencies. With B's line structure.
