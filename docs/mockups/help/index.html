Time

Every step has its own timer. Give it a preset; .done turns on when it runs out.

Playground 
init idle
-- start_btn
step fill 5s
  on valve
-- fill.done
step drain
  on drain_valve
-- after 3s -> idle
  • No problems
step fill 5spreset: 5 s (units ms s m h, 1m30s)
fill.donethe preset has elapsed (also fill.time: ms active)
-- after 3sthe step above has been active 3 s, no preset needed

Try this

  1. Make filling last 2 minutes.
  2. Stop filling early when level_high is on. 
    -- fill.done OR level_high
  3. Light busy_lamp while filling or draining.

Reference · Branches

select — one leg runs

select
  -- condition
  ...
|
  -- condition
  ...
end
Playground 
init wait
-- part_present
step measure 1s
select
  -- measure.done AND size_big
  step push_big
    on pusher_big
  -- big_out -> wait
|
  -- measure.done AND NOT size_big
  step push_small
    on pusher_small
  -- small_out -> wait
end
  • No problems

Rules

  • Follows a step; each leg starts with a transition.
  • Legs are tested left to right: the first true condition wins.
  • A leg ends with a transition: a jump (-> wait), or one that merges on the step after end.
  • At least two legs, separated by |.

Problems you may see

E211select must follow a stepexample
E212each leg must start with a transitionexample
E214legs that don't jump need a step after endexample
